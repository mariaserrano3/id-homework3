Tiramisu is an Italian dessert made with coffee, mascarpone, eggs, sugar and savoiardi biscuits. It does not need an oven, but it needs several hours in the fridge.

Prepare a strong coffee and let it cool. Whisk the egg yolks with the sugar and add the mascarpone until the cream is smooth. Dip the biscuits quickly in the coffee and arrange a layer in a dish, then cover with cream.

Repeat the layers and finish with cocoa powder. Keep the tiramisu in the fridge for at least six hours before serving.
