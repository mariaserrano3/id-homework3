Rome is the capital of Italy and one of the most visited cities in Europe. The Colosseum, the Roman Forum and the Pantheon are the best known monuments of the ancient city, and most of them can be visited on foot in a single day.

The neighborhood of Trastevere is famous for its narrow streets, small restaurants and lively evenings. Testaccio is the place to try traditional Roman food, and the Vatican is a short walk from the Prati district.

The metro has only three lines, so buses and trams are important. A weekly ticket is convenient for students who stay for a few months.
