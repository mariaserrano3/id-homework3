Spaghetti alla carbonara is one of the most famous dishes of Roman cuisine. The ingredients are spaghetti, eggs, pecorino romano cheese, guanciale and black pepper. Cream is never used.

Cut the guanciale into small strips and cook it slowly in a pan until the fat melts. Meanwhile boil the pasta in salted water. Mix the eggs with the grated pecorino and plenty of pepper.

Drain the pasta, put it in the pan with the guanciale away from the heat and add the egg mixture, stirring quickly. A little cooking water makes the sauce creamy without cream.
