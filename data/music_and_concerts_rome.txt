Rome has an intense cultural life. Large concerts are held in the Olympic Stadium and in the Circus Maximus during the summer, while smaller venues host jazz and rock nights throughout the year.

The Auditorium Parco della Musica is the main center for classical music and also hosts festivals. Many churches offer free organ concerts and choirs, and the opera season starts in the autumn.

Students can find discounted tickets, and street musicians play in the squares every evening.
