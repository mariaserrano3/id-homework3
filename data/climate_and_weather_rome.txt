The climate of Rome is Mediterranean. Summers are hot and dry, with temperatures that often exceed thirty five degrees in July and August, while winters are mild and rainy.

The best months to visit the city are April, May, September and October, when the temperature is pleasant and the crowds are smaller. In autumn the rain can be heavy for a few days, but it rarely lasts long.

Visitors should carry water, a hat and comfortable shoes, because the cobblestone streets are tiring to walk on.
