Data augmentation multiplies the training data by transforming the examples we already have. If a transformation does not change the label, training on the transformed example teaches the model to ignore what the transformation changes.

In natural language processing, simple augmentation techniques insert, delete, swap or replace words. In tabular data, SMOTE creates synthetic samples by interpolating between a minority class point and its nearest neighbors.

For entity resolution, two matching records can be mixed in a shared latent space and decoded back into text, producing a new synthetic record that still matches both originals. Augmentation also acts as a regularizer: a large model trained on few labels overfits, and the augmented copies smooth the decision boundary.
