The annual report of the company describes the results of the year 2025. Revenues grew by eight percent, driven by the new online services, while costs remained stable thanks to the automation of several processes.

The strategy for the next year is based on three priorities: data integration across the business units, a better use of customer data and the reduction of the energy consumption of the data centers.

The board thanks the employees and the shareholders for their support and confirms the investment plan for the next three years.
