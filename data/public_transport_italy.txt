Public transport in Italy is a mix of regional trains, high speed trains and local buses. The fast trains connect Rome with Milan, Florence and Naples in a few hours and tickets are cheaper when bought in advance.

Regional trains are slower but cheap, and the ticket must be validated before boarding. In Rome the metro, buses and trams share the same ticket, which is valid for one hundred minutes.

Strikes are frequent, so it is wise to check the schedule the day before the journey.
