Studying abroad in Rome is a great experience. The university offers many courses in English, but learning some Italian makes everyday life much easier, from shopping to talking with neighbors.

Finding a room can be difficult, so it is better to start searching early and to avoid paying any deposit before seeing the apartment. The student card gives discounts on public transport and museums.

Many Erasmus students meet in Trastevere and in the neighborhood of San Lorenzo. Do not forget to register at the university offices and to keep a copy of every document.
